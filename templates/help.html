Welcome, to the Bill Splitter App! Below are instructions to help navigate this webiste. if you need any further support or want to report a bug please contact: u5697255@live.warwick.ac.uk

How to create a bill for others to pay:

  • Go to your dashboard and press Add Bill
  • Fill in the details on the form and press submit
  • This bill will now be viewable by those that you sent it to and they will receive an email

    • How to mark a bill as paid that has been sent to you:

  • Click on the bill in the My Bills list which you have paid for
  • Submit proof of payment by submitting the image in the form
  • Submit the form by pressing the Pay Bill button

    • What's in the history page?

  • On the history page are old bils which you have sent to others that are now fully paid as well as bills which you yourself have paid
  • You can delete these off the page once you have acknowledged they are there to free up more space or keep them as a reminder
  • There is also a box which shows the amount of bills you have paid and the amount of bills that you have collected since the creation of your account
